Gesture Guide

Use simple eye movements and gestures to control WebEye-Access without using a keyboard or mouse.

01
👁

Blink

Close and open your eyes once to perform the primary selection action.

Action: Select / Click
02
👁️

Double Blink

Blink twice quickly to activate a menu or open an interactive interface element.

Action: Open Menu
03
←

Look Left

Move your eyes toward the left side of the screen to navigate to the previous option.

Action: Previous Option
04
→

Look Right

Move your eyes toward the right side of the screen to navigate to the next option.

Action: Next Option
05
↑

Look Up

Move your gaze toward the upper area of the screen to scroll the page upward.

Action: Scroll Up
06
↓

Look Down

Move your gaze toward the lower area of the screen to scroll the page downward.

Action: Scroll Down
